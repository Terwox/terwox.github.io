# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Research leaders, hiring managers, technical collaborators, and peers evaluating the depth and range of James Myers's work.

## Product Purpose

Present a concise, evidence-backed professional portfolio for a mixed-methods researcher and human-factors psychologist. The site should show both the research outcomes and the technical practice behind them, including software built or repaired in service of real problems.

## Brand Personality

Evidence-led, curious, technically credible.

## Anti-references

- Generic resume templates that flatten every accomplishment into the same visual weight.
- Developer dashboards that treat commit volume or lines of code as a proxy for impact.
- Software-as-a-service landing pages with inflated claims, ornamental metrics, or decorative interface chrome.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the professional identity: researcher first, builder as part of the research practice.
- Show evidence for claims through concrete projects, outcomes, and merged work.
- Explain technical work in terms of the problem it solved, not the amount of code written.
- Preserve the site's concise, direct voice and existing visual identity.
- Make every external claim traceable to a source.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 AA. Use semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, descriptive links, and layouts that remain readable on narrow screens and at increased text size.
